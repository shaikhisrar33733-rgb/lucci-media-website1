Meet Our Founder
Visionary & Founder

Lex Luciano

FOUNDER | @LUCCIMEDIA

Hello! I’m Lex Luciano, the founder and visionary behind Lucci Media. With over a decade of experience in the beauty and social media industries, I’ve combined my passion for creativity, branding, and strategy to help businesses elevate their online presence.

My journey began as a MAC makeup artist and licensed esthetician, where I honed my skills in enhancing beauty and building client trust. Over time, my expertise evolved into the digital space, working with globally recognized brands and growing my platforms organically.

At Lucci Media, I specialize in crafting innovative strategies, designing standout visuals, and managing social media for entrepreneurs and businesses looking to make a lasting impact. My goal is to help clients connect authentically with their audience, build their brand, and achieve measurable growth.

“When I’m not leading Lucci Media, I’m a mom and creator who thrives on balancing family life with entrepreneurial ambitions. I’m passionate about delivering exceptional results and empowering businesses to look and feel their best—online and beyond. Let’s work together to take your brand to the next level. Your vision deserves nothing less than excellence!”
